How it would work when your drawing is finished

You don't need to learn to code or redraw anything for a phone. You finish the cartoon the way you always do — the app is just the frame around it.

Step 1

You draw

Pencil, ink, watercolor — on paper. Take a clear photo or scan when you're happy with it.

Step 2

We drop it in

Each page becomes tap-through panels, sized for phones, with your titles, captions, and character names.

Step 3

Readers read

They open the app, pick an episode, and tap through panel by panel — and can follow you for the next one.

What readers get

  • A cozy place to read all your strips in order
  • New-episode alerts when you post
  • Character pages so they remember who's who
  • Favorites they can re-read and share

What you get

  • Your work in one place — not scattered across posts
  • A link you can put on your portfolio, eBay, and table at EBABZ
  • Room to grow: shop, behind-the-scenes, and contest pieces
  • Your name on it: Adams Red Ink, front and center

Questions you might have

Do I need the drawing finished to start?

No — that's the point of this example. The structure can be ready first, like an empty picture frame. Your finished drawing is the last piece that goes in.

Would my hand-drawn style stay hand-drawn?

Yes. The panels in this example are plain placeholders on purpose. In the real version they'd be photos/scans of your actual pencil, ink, and color — nothing redrawn by a computer.

Could this connect to my other work?

Yes — the same app could link out to your ACEO listings, your portfolio, and festival tables, so a reader who loves a strip can find the original art.
